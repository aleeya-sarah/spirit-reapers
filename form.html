Service Estimation Guidelines

Our service estimates are calculated based on the selected entity type, travel distance, additional services and requested response speed.


  • Ghost Removal Fee: Wandering Spirit (RM 100.00), Poltergeist (RM 200.00), and Long-term Haunting (RM 250.00).
  • Travel Fee: RM 1.50 per kilometre from our office. Accepted distance ranges from 1 to 500 km.
  • Property Size: Small (RM 150.00), Medium (RM 250.00), Large (RM 300.00).
  • Destination: Customers must provide their destination town or postcode.
  • Affected Area: Customers must identify the affected location: Bedroom, Living Room, Kitchen, Bathroom or Entire Property.
  • Additional Services: Salt Circle Seal (RM 25.00) and Blessing of the House (RM 35.00). These services are optional.
  • Delivery Speed: Standard (no additional charge), Express (+50%) or Midnight Call-out (+100%). The surcharge applies to the entire subtotal.

Note: Severity Level, Haunting Duration and Affected Area are collected for service assessment. They do not currently add extra charges to the estimated total.
